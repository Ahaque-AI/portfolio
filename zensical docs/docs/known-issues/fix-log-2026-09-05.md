# Introduction form required too much scrolling

## Symptom and cause

The owner reported that inputs/actions were below the initial viewport.
The form displayed every field, a large comment box, and both completion
messages beneath a tall introduction. Mobile devoted 325px to the hero alone.

## Resolution

Kept the approved visual identity and orbit behavior. Split entry into name
and contact steps, with comments collapsed initially, retained the name-only
path, added Back with preserved values, and replaced the form on completion.
Short viewports allocate less height to decorative regions. Scrolling remains
available when expanded content, a keyboard, or zoom needs it.

## Verification

`pnpm check`, `pnpm test` (five tests), and `pnpm build` passed. Validation tests
cover full and name-only requirements; the copy check guards against prohibited dashes.
Browser testing belongs to the owner. Manual checks: name-only on both steps,
Enter to advance, Back preserving values, expanding a note, agreement errors,
completion recovery, narrow screens, reduced motion, and keyboard visibility.
